Help Center

Answers to common questions about searching, listing a business, reviews, and your account.

Searching & browsing

How do I find a business on MeNim Ghana?

Use the search bar on the homepage or the Browse page to search by business name, category, or keyword. From Browse, narrow results with the category sidebar, region filter, star rating, or toggles like Verified, Open now, Has photos and Has videos.

Tap the location pin next to the search bar to sort results by real distance from where you are -- we'll ask your browser's permission first, and we only use your location to sort, never to hide a business whose saved address happens to be worded differently.

What does "Sort: Relevance" actually do?

Relevance ranks results by how well they match what you typed -- an exact or name-starts-with match ranks above a result that only contains your search term elsewhere, with star rating as the tie-breaker. You can switch to Rating, Most reviewed, Newest, A-Z or Nearest to me instead.

Can I save businesses to look at later?

Yes -- tap 🔖 Save on any listing while signed in. Saved businesses appear under "Saved Businesses" in your account menu and on your Profile page.

Listing & claiming a business

How do I list my business on MeNim Ghana?

Click "List your business" in the navigation bar and fill in your business details: name, category, location, hours, description, photos, and optionally a logo and a YouTube or Vimeo video link. New listings go into a pending queue and go live once approved.

Someone already listed my business -- how do I take it over?

Open the business's profile page and look for the "Claim this listing" option. This submits a claim request for review; once approved, the listing moves into your account and you get access to its owner dashboard -- profile views, call/WhatsApp lead counts, and the ability to reply to reviews.

What does the Verified badge mean?

A Verified badge means our team has checked the listing's key details. It's applied manually during moderation -- it isn't something a business can buy or switch on itself.

Can I see how many people view my listing or try to contact me?

Yes. Once you've claimed a listing, your Profile page's "My Business Listings" tab shows real profile-view counts and lead counts (phone and WhatsApp clicks combined), plus any reviews waiting for a reply.

Reviews

How do I leave a review?

Sign in, open the business's profile page, and use the review form. One review per person per business -- submitting again edits your existing review rather than creating a duplicate.

Can a business owner respond to a review?

Yes -- once a business is claimed, its owner can post a public reply to any review from either the business page or their owner dashboard on the Profile page.

I think a review is fake or abusive. What can I do?

Use the Report button beneath any review. Reported reviews go to our moderation queue for a human to check before any action is taken.

Account & security

How do I create an account?

Click Sign in, then "Create account," and sign up with your email and a password.

I forgot my password -- what now?

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. We'll email you a secure link to set a new one.

Does MeNim Ghana support two-factor authentication?

Yes. Under Profile > Account Settings > Security, you can turn on two-factor authentication using any authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.) -- a QR code and a manual setup key are both provided. Once enabled, signing in requires a 6-digit code from your app in addition to your password.
